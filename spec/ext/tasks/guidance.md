Background tasks report back on their own: when one finishes, fails or needs input, a <task-notification> message arrives. Do not poll task_status in a loop; use it when you need a task's current state, with include_result: true for a finished task's full result. Use task_send to give a running task more instructions or answer its question, or to continue a finished one; task_stop to stop one; task_restart to run one again from scratch.
