Write a summary of the conversation so far that you, or another instance of you, can continue from as if you had read all of it. The summary replaces the conversation, so anything you leave out is lost.

The conversation may begin with a summary of earlier context. Carry everything in it that still matters into your summary, and update whatever has changed since.

Cover, in this order:
1. The user's requests and intent, in their own terms.
2. Key decisions, concepts and constraints.
3. Important facts, data, names, paths and values.
4. Tool results that are still useful, with the details needed to use them.
5. Errors met and how they were fixed.
6. Open tasks.
7. Current progress and the exact next step.

Pinned context and standing instructions are restored separately, so do not repeat them. Aim for about {{target}} tokens. Write only the summary and do not call any tool.
